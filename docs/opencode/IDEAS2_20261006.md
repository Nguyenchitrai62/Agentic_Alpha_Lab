### 1. Monthly-expiry cash-carry (structural yield) [BOT+MANUAL]
- Mechanism: quarterly carry (oc_cashcarry, +0.21 %/mo at f=0.25) sat idle in 2025 (1/6 entries, basis<4%) because quarterlies rarely pay late-cycle; monthlies recycle premium 3x as often and harvest the same delivery convergence with shorter lockup, filling exactly the idle year.
- Rule (fixed): each Bybit/Binance monthly expiry, ENTER iff annualised ln(F/S)*365/DTE >= 4%/yr, f=0.25 per coin, hold to delivery, BTC+ETH only. No other parameter.
- Nearest closed: oc_cashcarry (quarterlies only) — differs: monthly tenors + idle-year fill, not another quarterly parameter.
- Data: `data/raw/qbasis_20261003` + monthly basis via same fetcher on `data/raw/bybit_quarterly_20261006` (monthlies in same archive).
- Test: structural sleeve accounting like oc_cashcarry (entry/delivery, fees 0.275% allocated, MtM DD, margin bound check vs oc_margin).
- Leakage: ENTER uses only F/S/DTE known at entry bar; exclude contracts delivering past last spot bar; never impute incomplete.
- Effect: +0.1-0.25 %/mo, ~0 DD; turns 2025-type years from ~0.01 into ~0.1.

### 2. Funding-settlement flat (deterministic cost saver) [BOT]
- Mechanism: gate funding taxes every long 0.01%/8h (~0.09 %/mo drag on longs); predicted funding is exactly reconstructible pre-settlement (MAE 0.05-0.07bp), and the settlement-minute move is untradable — so the only capturable edge is avoidance: flat longs over the 3 daily settlements when predicted pay is high.
- Rule (fixed): close book longs 5 min before each 00/08/16 UTC settlement iff predicted 8h rate > 0.03%, re-open at next 4h signal (limit, maker). Single threshold pre-registered.
- Nearest closed: oc_bookfunding (7d-regime tilt x0.75, NOT PROMISING) — differs: deterministic 5-min settlement window flat vs persistent regime tilt; v231 funding microstructure diagnostic (not a rule).
- Data: `data/raw/binance_premium_20260928` (premium klines + settled funding; scripts/fetch_binance_premium.py).
- Test: full 4-phase engine (book timing change) vs G2 base; report cost-saved row separately.
- Leakage: predicted rate must use only data <= T-5min (known previous rate + premium path); never the realised settlement rate.
- Effect: +0.03-0.08 %/mo, DD -0.1-0.3pp; small but near-free.

### 3. Stop-limit with taker fallback (slip saver) [BOT]
- Mechanism: oc_stopslip shows median market-open slip is negative (snapback) but the right tail is brutal (p99 +495/+738bps, FTX SOL 1347bps on Bybit); immediate market stops pay the whole tail. A stop-limit at the stop price captures the snapback median, with taker fallback next minute capping the tail.
- Rule (fixed): every stop becomes limit at stop price, valid 1 min; unfilled -> market at next minute open (taker). No distance parameter.
- Nearest closed: oc_stoptf (S15/S1 stop TIMING, both NO) — differs: order TYPE (limit+fallback) at same trigger, not timing; oc_stopslip was diagnostic only.
- Data: `data/raw/majors_intraday_20260924` + `data/raw/bybit_linear_1m_20261004` (both venues, 1m).
- Test: full 4-phase engine both venues (Bybit-priced run decides); stress with stop-slip S4 row.
- Leakage: fill needs 1m trade-through of limit in the exit minute; no OHLC queue claims; stop-first on same-bar SL/TP ties.
- Effect: +0.05-0.15 %/mo, DD -0.2-0.5pp; mainly tail insurance.

- Rule (fixed): unfilled book order at minute 30 is cancelled/replaced once at mid-10bps (post-only, rest to minute 60). One re-peg, fixed minute.
- Nearest closed: oc_bookoffset (vol-scaled OFFSET distance, PROMISING-but-closed) — differs: intrabar re-price TIMING at fixed distance, not distance scaling; oc_cadence (slower decisions) is the opposite direction.
- Data: same 1m as #3; no new data.
- Test: full 4-phase engine (book execution change); report fill-rate + maker-share + cost rows.
- Leakage: re-peg price from 1m data <= minute 30 only; minute-5 rule still bans fills in first 5 min after close.
- Effect: +0.05-0.12 %/mo via fills; DD roughly flat.
