## A. The 28 anomalies (mechanism | best source | reported effect + holding period)

1. Time-series momentum, 1-4 weeks. Winners keep winning over 1-4 weeks (underreaction / trend-chasing
   + leveraged-long demand). Source: Liu & Tsyvinski "Risks and Returns of Cryptocurrency" (RFS 2021,
   NBER w24877 2018, https://www.nber.org/papers/w24877) + Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu "Common Risk Factors in
   Cryptocurrency" (JF 2022, https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13119). Effect: top-quintile 11.22 %/wk vs
   bottom 2.60 %/wk; 1-sd weekly return -> +3.16 % next week (coef 0.19); XS 2wk-form/1wk-hold WML
   3.18 %/wk (risk-managed 3.47 %/wk, Sharpe 1.12 -> 1.42). Holding: 1-4 weeks.
2. Short-term (daily) cross-sectional reversal. Illiquid coins reverse the prior day (liquidity
   provision compensation); the most liquid coins show momentum instead. Source: Zaremba et al "Up or
   down? Short-term reversal, momentum, and liquidity effects in cryptocurrency markets" (IRFA 2021,
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2021.101884). Intraday sibling: Kitron & Wengrowicz "Short-horizon
   mean reversion in cryptocurrency markets" (arXiv 2608.21888, 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21888):
   15-min sign-flip 50.2 -> 53.0 %, AUC gap +0.031 (+0.011 conservative), gross ~1.3 bp/trade vs ~5 bp
   cost. Holding: 1 day (XS) / 15 min (intraday).
3. Intraday momentum, first-half-hour -> last-half-hour (BTC). Volume-clock day: high-volume morning
   session predicts the last half-hour (liquidity provision, not late-informed trading). Source: Shen,
   Urquhart & Wang "Bitcoin intraday time series momentum" (Financial Review 2022,
   https://doi.org/10.1111/fire.12290; equity origin Gao-Han-Li-Zhou 2018, https://ssrn.com/abstract=2552752).
   Effect: slope 0.968, t 4.38, R2 1.44 %; strongest on high-volume/volatile/downturn days; market-timing
   gains especially in BTC downturns. Holding: intraday (one session).
4. Overnight vs intraday / US-hours segmentation. On NYSE-open days BTC gains accrue overnight
   (stocks closed); weekends flip; the 21:00-23:00 UTC window (all equity markets closed) is the
   strongest. Source: Padysak & Vojtko "Seasonality, Trend-following, and Mean Reversion in Bitcoin"
   (2022, summary https://blog.paperswithbacktest.com/p/bitcoin-never-sleeps-exploiting-seasonality).
   Effect: buy 21:00 UTC / sell 23:00 UTC daily ~33 % annualised, lower vol/DD than buy-and-hold;
   "at MAX" 10d-high momentum also beats holding. Holding: 2 h nightly / days (MAX rule).
5. Day-of-week / weekend. Monday returns significantly higher (BTC); weekend volatility lower but
   spreads/volumes thinner; Monday+Thursday volatility high. Sources: Caporale & Plastun "The day of
   the week effect in the cryptocurrency market" (CESifo 2017/FRL 2019,
   https://www.ifo.de/DocDL/cesifo1_wp6716.pdf); Aharon & Qadan day-of-week BTC return+vol (2019);
   Baur et al "Bitcoin time-of-day, day-of-week and month-of-year effects" (FRL 2019,
   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2019.04.023). Effect: Monday mean excess positive (BTC only; LTC/XRP/DASH
   mostly none); Monday long-and-close same-day simulation positive but mostly inseparable from random
   trading. Holding: 1 day.
6. Turn-of-month (TOM). Last-day-plus-first-3-days window earns excess (month-end flows / salary /
   window-dressing analogue). Sources: "Turn-of-the-month effect in cryptocurrencies" (BTC/ETH/LTC
   2015-2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbef.2022.100724 abs); Vasileiou "Is the Turn of the Month an
   Anomaly...? Evidence from Bitcoin and Ethereum" (SSRN 2022, https://ssrn.com/abstract=4230561);
   Qadan-Aharon-Eichel "Seasonal and Calendar Effects" (EM 2022): TOM in BTC, within-month common to all
   coins. Effect: TOM-timing beats buy-and-hold +21.77 %/yr (BTC), +47.10 %/yr (LTC) in-sample;
   better risk-reward even when raw does not win. Holding: ~4 days/month.
7. Funding-rate carry (perp long pays / short earns). Persistent positive funding = leveraged-long
   willingness to pay; short perp + long spot harvests it. Sources: Schmeling-Schrimpf-Todorov "Crypto
   Carry" (BIS WP 1087, 2023 / Management Science 2026, https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry.pdf);
   Christin et al "The Crypto Carry Trade" (2022, https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/azj/files/CarryTrade.v1.0.pdf):
   in-sample Sharpe 7-12.8, median funding 0.01 %/8h (~11 %/yr); "Funding rate arbitrage on CEX and DEX"
   (2026). Effect: futures-leg mean 2-4 %/mo; carry up to 40-60 % p.a. in booms. Holding: rolling / to delivery.
8. Futures basis carry (spot vs dated futures). Same-expiry convergence + segmented margin; basis is the
   strongest XS futures predictor. Sources: Schmeling et al (as #7); Chi "An empirical investigation on
   risk factors in cryptocurrency futures" (J Futures Markets 2023, https://doi.org/10.1002/fut.22425):
   basis L-S 329 %/yr (t 7.10), momentum 89 % (t 1.71), basis-momentum 236 % (t 5.20), 5d lookback/1d hold;
   Gornall-Rinaldi-Xiao "Perpetual Futures and Basis Risk" (SSRN 2024, https://ssrn.com/abstract=5036933).
   Effect: 2-3 %/mo futures-leg mean, vol ~25 %/mo, negative skew. Holding: days to expiry / 1d XS hold.
9. Volatility-managed portfolios. Scale by 1/lag-realised-variance (vol timing; variance forecastable,
   returns not proportionally so). Source: Moreira & Muir "Volatility-Managed Portfolios" (JF 2017,
   https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.12513; NBER w22208): alphas large, appraisal ratios 0.33-0.91, market
   utility +65 %. Crypto application: "Cryptocurrency momentum has (not) its moments" (2025,
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s11408-025-00474-9): 8w-vol-managed 1.86 %/wk (5 % signif) vs plain 0.71 %/wk;
   market return 1.59 %/wk. Holding: 1 wk-month rebalance.
10. MAX / lottery preference. High maximum-daily-return coins overpriced by skewness-seekers (stocks:
    negative MAX-return). Crypto sign REVERSED: high-MAX earns MORE. Sources: Bali-Cakici-Whitelaw
    "Maxing out" (JFE 2011, https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~rwhitela/papers/max%20jfe11.pdf); Ozdamar et al
    "Lottery-like preferences and the MAX effect in the cryptocurrency market" (Fin Innov 2021,
    https://doi.org/10.1186/s40854-021-00291-9): Hi-Lo MAX decile +3.03 %/wk raw (+1.99 % risk-adj);
    Li et al "MAX momentum in cryptocurrency markets" (IRFA 2021). Holding: 1 wk.
11. Liquidity / Amihud. Illiquid coins earn more (XS) but are less efficient; liquidity predicts
    efficiency (Hurst), not always a premium. Sources: Zhang & Li "Liquidity risk and expected
    cryptocurrency returns" (IJFE 2023, https://doi.org/10.1002/ijfe.2431): negative liquidity-return
    relation (Amihud), robust to size screens; Brauneis & Mestel "Liquidity and market efficiency in
    cryptocurrencies" (EL 2018): predictability diminishes in liquid coins, no illiquidity premium in
    456-coin sort; "Maturation of Bitcoin microstructure 2012-2025" (2026): realised Amihud collapsed.
    Holding: 1d-1wk XS tilt.
12. Size (SMB). Small caps earn more (information/liquidity). Source: Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu JF 2022 (as #1):
    market+size+momentum span the XS; 2023-24 long-short bottom/top-quartile size +1.21 %/wk after
    migration fix ("Crypto Pricing with Hidden Factors" replication, arXiv 2026). Holding: 1 wk XS.
13. Network / on-chain value (NVT, price-to-new-addresses, MVRV). High valuation vs network activity
    predicts LOW future returns (crypto "value" effect); MVRV-z timing beats holding; USDT-on-exchange
    predicts +returns. Sources: Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu "Accounting for Cryptocurrency Value" (2021,
    https://cowles.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2022-10/onchain_v18.pdf): value-effect alpha 1 % signif
    vs C-3; "Using on-chain data to predict Bitcoin cycles" (2026, NUPL/MVRV-z/CVDD: MVRV timing Sharpe
    ~1.28, CVDD bottom p ~99 %); Chi-Chu-Hao "Return and Volatility Forecasting Using On-Chain Flows"
    (arXiv 2411.06327): USDT-inflow -> +BTC/ETH 1-6h, ETH-inflow -> -ETH. Holding: 1h-6h (flows) / cycle (MVRV).
14. Exchange flows / reserves. Coins loaded onto exchanges = selling pressure (negative future returns);
    reserves beat all prior predictors horse-race. Sources: "Loaded for bear: Bitcoin private wallets,
    exchange reserves and prices" (Journal of Banking & Finance 2022, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426622002023);
    Chi-Chu-Hao (as #13): ETH net inflow -> -returns all 1-6h; BTC inflow ~no return power, -vol.
    Program-side: whale perp taker-flow members W2/O1. Holding: 1h-1d.
15. Stablecoin mint / supply. USDT mints lift BTC 5-30 min (FOMO asymmetry: burns do nothing; stronger
    with positive sentiment + Whale Alert tweet); Tether jumps predict -BTC next day. Sources: Saggu
    "The Intraday Bitcoin Response to Tether Minting and Burning Events" (Finance Research Letters 2022; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.05232); Wei "The impact of Tether
    grants on Bitcoin" (EL 2018, +returns after grants); Grobys-Huynh "When Tether says JUMP!" (2022):
    positive Tether jump + 1 % Tether return -> BTC -3.65 % to -8.49 % daily. Holding: 5-30 min (mint) / 1d (jumps).
16. Google / investor attention. Attention Granger-predicts 1-2 wk returns (BTC); bidirectional
    flows to 6d, vol->attention larger. Sources: Liu & Tsyvinski RFS 2021 (as #1): 1-sd Twitter count ->
    +2.50 % 1-wk BTC; Drobetz-Momtaz-Schröder; Sublime et al "The link between Bitcoin and Google Trends
    attention" (2021, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.07104): GTC index <-> BTC bidirectional to 6 lags;
    "Investor attention in cryptocurrency markets" (2021): +attention -> +return/+vol/+illiquidity.
    Holding: 1d-2wk.
17. Twitter / social sentiment. Sentiment predicts only very short horizons (<= 15 min); happiness
    predicts extremes; dispersion predicts vol. Sources: Naeem et al "Does investor sentiment on social
    media provide robust information for Bitcoin returns predictability?" (FRL 2021,
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.frl.2020.101494): sentiment -> returns only <= 15 min; Bouteska "Predictive
    power of investor sentiment for Bitcoin returns" (2022): sentiment index strong short-term predictor;
    "On the predictive power of tweet sentiments" (VADER SD/IA). Holding: minutes-1d.
18. Options skew / variance risk premium. ATM IV > realised vol on average (sellers earn VRP ~14 % var
    units BTC vs ~2 % SPX); skew (RR25) marks stress; higher moments predict returns beyond variance.
    Sources: Alexander & Imeraj "The Bitcoin VIX and its variance risk premium" (SSRN 2019);
    Almeida et al "Risk Premia in the Bitcoin Market" (arXiv 2410.15195, 2024): BVIX2 0.72 vs BRV 0.58,
    VRP 0.14; Atanasova et al "What Do Crypto Options Tell Us?" (SSRN 2026); Chappe "Evaluating the
    Volatility Risk Premium for Crypto Options" (2023, practitioner summary). Holding: 7d-1mo options.
19. Jump risk. Jumps frequent, cluster, predicted by order-flow imbalance/aggressive share/spread; VPIN
    predicts jumps with persistent FEVD share; jumps lower future variance (signed). Sources: Scaillet et
    al "High-Frequency Jump Analysis of the Bitcoin Market" (2017, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.08175):
    OF imbalance + aggressive share + spread predict jumps; Chaim-Laurini "Volatility and return jumps in
    bitcoin" (EL 2018); "Bitcoin wild moves: order flow toxicity and price jumps" (2025, VPIN->jumps VAR);
    "Bitcoin volatility predictability — jumps and regimes" (MIDAS jump improves forecasts, Black-Swan
    periods). Holding: minutes-1d (jump trade) / vol forecast 1d-1wk.
20. Cross-exchange lead-lag / arbitrage (incl. Coinbase premium, Kimchi). Segmented venues diverge
    (15 % avg US-Korea Dec17-Feb18, 40 % peaks, $2B left); common signed volume explains 80-85 % of BTC
    returns; idiosyncratic legs explain spreads; Coinbase premium tracks US institutional bid. Sources:
    Makarov & Schoar "Trading and Arbitrage in Cryptocurrency Markets" (JFE 2020,
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.07.001); Borri-Shakhnov cross-section; CoinGlass/CryptoQuant
    Coinbase Premium Index + Kimchi series (practitioner, free history). Holding: hours-days (arbitrage) /
    4h-1wk (premium as context).
21. CME gap / CME-spot basis. Weekend CME close -> Friday-Sunday spot drift leaves gaps (e.g. ~$10k Mar

    https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2026/aligning-cryptocurrency-derivatives-with-spot-markets-measuring-the-247-trading-opportunity.html);
    "Pricing Efficiency and Arbitrage in Bitcoin Spot and Futures" (2019); Kapar-Olmo price discovery

22. Halving cycle. Pre-halving attention buying then reversal (CAAR [-5,5] -7.55 %); cycle-four break

    (SSRN 2026, https://ssrn.com/abstract=6589402): cycle-4 [-30,+90] -41.5 %, [-30,+365] -19.6 % vs
    cycle-3 +172.2 %; SPY beta ~1.04, R2 0.17; "Bitcoin Runs on a Clock" (arXiv 2607.26188). Holding:
    months (cycle regime).
23. BTC-dominance rotation / altseason. BTC.D ceiling (~48 % 2021-23, ~55-60 % 2025-26) then alt

    "Bitcoin's Price Alchemy: FOMC and CPI" (SSRN 2023, https://ssrn.com/abstract=4478184): massive

    OI-cohort notes (2024, practitioner); program DATA LEADERBOARD (positioning +0.12 in 2023 only).

    daily dFGI does NOT Granger-cause returns (returns -> sentiment); fear stuck 30 %+ of 2025-26.
    Sources: "Do bitcoin returns move sentiment? Evidence from the crypto fear & greed index" (2026,
    https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S305070062600006X): dFGI no-Granger, no OOS gain; Wang "U-shaped FGI vs
    synchronicity" (2024); alternative.me/Coinglass history (free daily since 2018). Holding: 1d-1wk

    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2023.e14236). Holding: minutes (HFT).
