## B. NEW IDEAS (exactly 10, ranked by expected value / cost)

### B1. Cross-venue funding-spread satellite book member (Hyperliquid − Binance)
Hypothesis: when HL and Binance disagree violently on funding, the tape is segmented/crowded and
the book should listen to the disagreement, not the level.
Mechanism: 7d HL-minus-Binance funding spread per coin, z-scored on a trailing 1y window; a
v290-style 20 % satellite member (annual + quarterly, pooled HGB, same embargo) added to CB books.
Data: D4 (+ D3 EAPI funding cross-check). Pre-reg (≤3): S1 spread-z member at 20 %; S2 S1 with
sign cap (|z|>3 clipped); S3 S1 long-leg only. Judge: v290 screen first (IC gain 3/4 dev years to
earn an engine run), then full 4-phase engine vs G2 on 2021-2024, 2025 scored once.
Effect: +0.0-0.15 %/mo if real, DD flat/down; costs: member turnover within existing book cadence.
Leakage: funding histories timestamped per venue, merged as-of bar close; quantiles fit pre-anchor
+ 7d embargo; HL covers 2023-2024 only → legs judged on overlap + paper.
Prior: 20 % (positioning helped only 2023 in DATA LEADERBOARD; disagreement may be the robust form).

### B2. ETF-outflow book throttle (primary-market overhang gate)
Hypothesis: record spot-ETF outflows precede multi-day spot-supply pressure that the perp-only book
buys into.
Mechanism: 5d-cumulative BTC+ETH net flow < trailing-1y p5 → book longs x0.5 that week (dip sleeve
untouched); re-open at next 4h signal. Data: D7. Pre-reg: T1 p5/1y; T2 p10/1y (more events,
weaker gate). Judge: screen on 2024-2025 overlap (no selection — short span), then 4-phase engine
vs G2 reported as overlap-only + paper log; never a dev4 selection input.
Effect: +0.0-0.1 %/mo, DD −0.0-0.3pp; ~5-10 gate-weeks/yr so wide CI. Costs ~0 (fewer fills).
Leakage: flow table dated T+1 morning, applied from the first 4h bar after publication; thresholds
fit pre-2024. Prior: 12 % (only ~2 events-clusters/yr; 2025-type idle years benefit most).

### B3. Stablecoin net-creation impulse book gate (dry-powder regime)
Hypothesis: sustained stablecoin creation = incoming risk-bid; destruction = risk-off; the book
should be longer into creation and shorter into destruction.
Mechanism: 30d USDT+USDC net-creation z (trailing-2y norm): longs x1.0 if z > 0.5, x0.75 if z < −1
(shorts unchanged — hedge kept per v397 lesson). Data: D8 live + `onchain_20260924/stablecoins.csv`
for history. Pre-reg: G1 (0.5/−1.0); G2 (0/−1.5, asymmetric). Judge: full 4-phase engine (book
timing change) on 2021-2024, 2025 once.
Effect: +0.0-0.12 %/mo, DD −0.0-0.3pp. Leakage: daily caps known after day-end, applied D+1;
norm window ends pre-anchor + embargo. Prior: 15 % (nearest closed `oc_usdtprem` was price premium,
not supply; supply moves slower and may mark, not cause, risk-on).

### B4. Four-phase agreement trim (anti-crowding governor)
Hypothesis: half of G2's DD is the book's one-way concentration (`oc_ddanat_g2`, `g2_dd`); when all
4 phase-clocks agree 4/4 on direction, the portfolio is maximally crowded into the grind window.
Mechanism: book target x0.75 on bars where all 4 phases hold the same net sign (dip sleeve
unchanged). No new data. Pre-reg: A1 x0.75 on 4/4; A2 x0.5 on 4/4. Judge: 4-phase engine vs G2,
2021-2024 + 2025 once; must also beat an exposure-matched constant-0.9 control
(`oc_premexpo` lesson).
Effect: −0.1..+0.05 %/mo, DD −0.5-1.5pp — a DD buy, not return; fails cleanly if agreement marks
trend (then A1 loses return 4/5 and closes). Leakage: phase signs are engine-state at the close,
no future data. Prior: 18 % for DD reduction, 5 % for return gain.

### B5. Maintenance-window flat (exchange outage-risk rule)
Hypothesis: holding book/dip risk through announced matching-engine maintenance is uncompensated
gap risk (`oc_outage`: routine cost 1-2.5 %, tail only in flushes).
Mechanism: from the forward-collected maintenance calendar (D9), flat book longs and cancel resting
dip bids ±2h around Binance/Bybit announced windows; re-enter at next signal (limit, maker).
Data: D9. Pre-reg: W1 ±2h flat; W2 W1 + skip new dip bids 4h before. Judge: 4-phase engine vs G2 on
history where windows are known (announcement archive backfill) + forward paper; effect reported
per-event and 5y.
Effect: ~0 return (±0.02), DD −0.0-0.2pp, tail insurance only; ~5-15 events/yr.
Leakage: windows known at announcement time, applied strictly after; never infer windows from price.
Prior: 25 % for "costs nothing", 5 % for measurable DD gain.

### B6. OKX liquidation-pulse conditional extra rung (forced-selling depth)
Hypothesis: the dip ladder's fixed depths miss forced-selling cascades visible on a second venue;
one extra 4.5sg bid exactly during liquidation pulses buys the cascade, not the calendar.
Mechanism: if trailing-24h OKX liquidation notional per coin > trailing-1y p90 at the 4h close,
add one 4.5sg rung (base size, TP 1.0sg, same stops) for the next bar only. Data: D10 (index

Judge: dip replica + placebo gate (full PROMISING legs + dSum5y ≥ +0.273) first; engine only if passed.
Effect: +0.0-0.1 %/mo, DD +0.0-0.3pp (extra depth can deepen DD — gate decides).
Leakage: liq flow strictly ≤ close; depths from sigma360 ≤ close; minute-5 ban kept.
Prior: 10 % (nearest closed `oc_expirydip` managed +0.0164 vs +0.273; pulses may be the better trigger).

poor record — `oc_idea7`, `oc_regime`).
