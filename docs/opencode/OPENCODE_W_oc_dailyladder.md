Rule (fixed, no grid): at each UTC day open D (00:00), for each major c: sigma_d = std (ddof 1) of daily open-to-open simple returns over the 90 days ending D-1 (min 60); resting limit bids at O_D x (1 - k sigma_d) for k in {1.5, 2.0, 2.5}, valid from 00:05 to 23:55 of day D; fill on trade-through (low < level x (1 - 0.0005)), maker 0.0002; each filled rung: TP limit at level x (1 + 1.0 sigma_d) (maker), close-stop at level x (1 - 2.0 sigma_d) checked on HOURLY closes (exit next hour open, taker 0.00055), time exit at the open of day D+3 (taker); longs pay 0.0001 per 8h settlement held. Use research/tournament/ext/hourly_ext.parquet (hourly OHLC; within an hour where both TP and stop could trigger assume STOP first; the fill hour cannot also take profit). Unit size per rung. Report per anchor year (2021-09-24 + k*365 d): fills, win rate, mean net bps, sum, worst day, maxDD of the daily-sum path, and the daily-P&L correlation with the 4h BOT dip stream (fills_U_ext majors x R2 depths, y_dep as research/tournament/oc_idea7 loads it, daily sums by exit date). PROMISING only if the daily sleeve's sum > 0 in >= 4/5 years AND its correlation with the 4h stream < 0.5 AND (4h + 0.5 x daily) maxDD per year is not worse than 4h alone scaled to the same total sum in >= 3/5 years.
