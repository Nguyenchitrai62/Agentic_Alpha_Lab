Problem: bot_parity_adopt arm C (bot --dip-gross-cap 2.0) returned +1.12 % vs +3.58 % without the cap on 2026-09-01..23, while the ENGINE G2 cap costs ~nothing over 5 years. The bot cap is stricter than the engine: it counts RESTING bids and allocates the room cumulatively shallow-first (dropping deeper bids), whereas the engine only cuts a rung at its FILL minute to (G - notional of rungs open at that minute).
Fix (engine-faithful): with the cap on, every resting dip bid of a phase is sized min(its normal size, room) where room = G x sub equity - notional of OPEN filled dip pieces of that phase (NOT minus other resting bids); recompute every cycle so after a fill the remaining bids shrink. Document the residual risk (several bids filling inside one cycle can exceed G by at most the sum of their sizes, which the engine also cannot see within a minute) and keep a hard safety bound: total open + resting dip notional <= 2 x G x sub equity. Tests for the new allocation. Then rerun run_adopt.py arms A (no cap) and C (cap 2.0, adopt off and on) with the fixed bot and report returns / fills vs the engine (+7.10 %).
